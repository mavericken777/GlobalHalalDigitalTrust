## 1. Purpose

Establish a non-exclusive facilitation framework under which CODA may introduce qualified Chinese industrial, manufacturing and project counterparts to GHSC for evaluation against a Halal / Tayyib evidence corridor (China origin → nominated destination markets), and GHSC may present corridor requirements to those counterparts.

## 2. Scope (only this)

2.1 Introductions of candidate manufacturers, parks, and project owners.  
2.2 Joint workshops / briefing sessions at CODA or member venues.  
2.3 A written pipeline list, updated monthly during the Term, of introduced parties and status (`introduced / under review / declined / pilot candidate`).  
2.4 No logistics operation, no laboratory operation, no retail listing, and no certification decision is in this paper.

## 3. Roles

**CODA:** institutional network facilitation; member introductions; venue and protocol support; no warranty that an introduced enterprise will qualify or ship.  
**GHSC:** corridor evidence requirements; coordination with competent Halal authorities **as a commercial coordinator only**; pilot design. GHSC does not issue SPHM.

## 4. Authority firewall

Nothing in this MoA grants either Party governmental, certification, customs or religious authority. Malaysian Halal decisions remain with the competent Malaysian authorities. Chinese regulatory decisions remain with the competent Chinese authorities. Destination import decisions remain with the destination competent authorities.

## 5. Commercials

This MoA does not set fees, exclusivity, or revenue share. Any introduction fee or joint programme cost sits in **Annex C**, signed separately.

## 6. Pipeline and data

Introduced-party contact data is confidential. Neither Party uploads the other’s member list into a public repo, pitch deck, or press note without written consent.

## 7. Publicity

No use of the other Party’s name, logo, or any government mark except by prior written approval of the exact artwork and sentence.

## 8. Term

Two (2) years from the last date of signing, unless ended earlier by 30 days’ written notice.

## 9. Legal character

Statements of intent. **Binding only:** clause 6 (confidentiality), clause 7 (publicity), clause 10 (notices), clause 11 (governing law). Not a partnership, JV, or agency.
