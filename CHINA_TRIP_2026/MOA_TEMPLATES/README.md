# How to use these templates on the trip

1. Print **two** English copies per meeting. Do not print Chinese until counsel has read exclusivity and governing-law lines.
2. Fill legal name + unified social credit / CR number **in the room** before anyone chops.
3. Annex A must contain at least one named site, method, SKU family, or document list. Empty annex = do not sign.
4. Photograph the chopped page immediately. Log it in `../FIELD_LOG.md`.
5. Send the scan to counsel the same day. Do not announce the counterparty on social or in a deck until clause 7 publicity is cleared.

Retailer papers (Lulu / Carrefour / Tamimi / noon) are **cold templates**. There is no existing relationship in this repo. Treat first contact as a buyer meeting, not a signing ceremony.
