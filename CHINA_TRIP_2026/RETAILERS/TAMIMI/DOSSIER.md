# Tamimi Markets — official trip dossier

## Company

- Banner: **Tamimi Markets**  
- Vehicle to confirm: **Tamimi Markets Company**  
- HQ / office commonly cited: Al Rakka Al Janubiya, Al Khobar, PO Box 6941; group roots in Dammam  
- Family group: Tamimi Group of Companies (Sheikh Ali Abdullah Al-Tamimi line)  
- Retail since 1979; public materials describe Tamimi as a **direct importer**  
- Feb 2024: completion of 100% acquisition of **Al Raya For Food Stuffs Company JSC** (western KSA store base)

## Why this banner is on the trip

KSA-native supermarket group. Direct import culture. Al Raya adds Jeddah / west-coast receiving. If the SKU is for KSA households rather than tourist hypermarkets, this is the right second conversation after Lulu KSA.

## First ask

Procurement + quality in Khobar or Dammam. Name whether the PO sits on Tamimi Markets Company or another Tamimi commercial company. Do not address Al Raya unless they say the west-coast file lives there.

## Destination file (KSA)

Buyer will typically care about SFDA import listing for the category, Arabic label, and the Halal body **they** accept. GHSC does not pick that body for them.

## Red lines

Do not treat Tamimi Group construction / oilfield companies as the grocery buyer. Do not promise SFDA listing. Do not use “Made in Saudi” partnership language for a China-origin SKU.
