Control date: 2026-09-26  
Purpose: field brief for meetings in China. This folder is the **operating checklist**, not a press release.

## What you can say in the room

- GHSC HK exists (CR 79801544, incorporated 11 Feb 2026).
- Sinotrans Overseas has **chopped a non-binding 2-year MoU** dated 3 Mar 2026 (HK law). Confidentiality binds. Price, exclusivity, revenue share do **not**.
- JGC and Sinotrans already have a **separate non-binding** Sep 2025 framework. It is **not** automatically novated onto GHSC.
- PHC–JGC MoU (27 Aug 2025) is **non-binding JV intent**. PHC is a Perak GLC. It is **not** JAKIM.
- NICFS / Hengqin food-safety platform is a **China food-safety / anti-counterfeit** pitch. It is **not** a Halal certificate and does not replace JAKIM / SFDA / GSO.
- This GitHub repo is the **evidence and decision-support library**. It is **not** a live multi-region platform and does **not** issue SPHM.

## What you cannot say

- “We are exclusive logistics partner.”
- “Green Lane is approved.”
- “AmanahGraph is live on Sinotrans yards.”
- “PHC / GHSC issues JAKIM certificates.”
- “Shipment 001 has sailed.”
- Market size as a single number without source (`$2.3T` vs corridor `$10B` class claims — treat as **unreconciled marketing** until a cited source is attached).

## Walk-out rule

A meeting is a success only if you leave with **one** of:

1. named warehouse + named operating unit + visit date, or
2. a priced service term sheet / SLA draft, or
3. a data-sharing protocol initialled, or
4. a written “no” / deferral with a named next owner.

A second MoU with empty commercials is **not** a win.
