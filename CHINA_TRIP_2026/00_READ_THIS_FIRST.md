## What you cannot say

- “We are exclusive logistics partner.”
- “Green Lane is approved.”
- “AmanahGraph is live on Sinotrans yards.”
- “This MoU *is* a JAKIM certificate.”
- “Shipment 001 has sailed.”
- Market size as a single number without source (`$2.3T` vs corridor `$10B` class claims — treat as **unreconciled marketing** until a cited source is attached).

## Walk-out rule

A meeting is a success only if you leave with **one** of:

1. named warehouse + named operating unit + visit date, or
2. a priced service term sheet / SLA draft, or
3. a data-sharing protocol initialled, or
4. a written “no” / deferral with a named next owner.

A second MoU with empty commercials is **not** a win.
