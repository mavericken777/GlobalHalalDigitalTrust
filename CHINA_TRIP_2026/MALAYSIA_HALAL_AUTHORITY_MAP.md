# Malaysia Halal authority map — official trip brief

Control date: 2026-09-26  
This page corrects earlier shorthand that flattened Perak State and the Rulers into “PHC is not JAKIM.” That sentence was incomplete.

## Constitutional facts (say these in the room)

1. **Islam is the religion of the Federation** (Federal Constitution, Article 3). Other religions may be practised in peace and harmony.
2. **Each Malay Ruler is the Head of the religion of Islam in His State.** In Perak, that is Duli Yang Maha Mulia Sultan of Perak.
3. **The Yang di-Pertuan Agong is the Head of the religion of Islam** for the Federal Territories, and is the King for the whole nation on a rotational basis among the Rulers.
4. Islamic law and the administration of the religion of Islam are **State matters** (Ninth Schedule), except in the Federal Territories.
5. In Perak, **Majlis Agama Islam dan Adat Melayu Perak (MAIAMP)** aids and advises the Sultan on the religion of Islam and is the chief authority in the State after the Sultan, as provided in the Administration of the Religion of Islam (Perak) Enactment 2004.
6. **Jabatan Agama Islam Perak (JAIPK)** — including Bahagian Pengurusan Halal — is the State department that administers Halal applications, premise inspection and monitoring on the ground, through the national system (**MYeHALAL**) and national manuals.

## One regulatory set, nationwide

Malaysia does **not** run 13 different Halal standards.

| Layer | Who | What |
|---|---|---|
| Apex religious authority in the State | Sultan of Perak (and the other Rulers in their States; YDPA nationally / FT) | Head of Islam |
| State religious council | MAIAMP | Aids and advises the Sultan |
| State religious department | JAIPK / JAIN equivalent in each State | Field administration, including Halal files via MYeHALAL |
| Federal Islamic development & national Halal scheme | **JAKIM** (Jabatan Kemajuan Islam Malaysia), including Bahagian Pengurusan Halal / Majlis Halal Malaysia | National procedures (MPPHM), national Halal logo / SPHM scheme, international recognition, coordination |
| National standards text | Department of Standards Malaysia / JSM (MS 1500, MS 2400 series, …) | One MS set used nationwide |
| State industry GLC | **Perak Halal Corporation Sdn Bhd** | Perak State Government vehicle for the Halal **industry** — local and global investment, parks, trade, facilitation |

Federal and State Islamic machinery are designed to operate as **one Halal regulatory system**. JAKIM is the federal backbone and the face of the national scheme abroad. State Sultans, MAIAMP and JAIPK are not “optional extras.” A China counterpart who only hears “JAKIM in Putrajaya” has been briefed badly.

## Where Perak Halal Corporation sits

PHC is the **Perak State Government GLC for the Halal industry**, domestic and international. That is an industrial and trade mandate: parks, investors, training, market access, facilitation of compliance with the **same** JAKIM / MS / MPPHM rules that apply everywhere in Malaysia.

JAKIM’s own portal records a 8 May 2019 courtesy call by PHC (then led by CEO Abdul Aziz bin Abd Rahim) to the Secretariat of Majlis Halal Malaysia — presentation of Perak’s Halal development plan and discussion of cooperation. That is the correct relationship: **State industry GLC working with the federal Halal secretariat**, not a private trader borrowing the JAKIM name.

Perak public Halal administration (JAIPK Bahagian Pengurusan Halal, Ipoh) runs applications through MYeHALAL. That is the State arm of the **same** national scheme.

## What you may say to Chinese counterparties

- Malaysia practises **one** Halal regulatory set, determined nationally (JAKIM procedures + MS standards) and administered with the States and the Rulers.
- Perak is an Islamic State of the Federation. The Sultan is the Head of Islam in Perak.
- PHC is the Perak State Government’s Halal industry corporation for local and global work.
- Certification and the national logo remain inside the JAKIM / State religious-department scheme (MYeHALAL / SPHM). PHC **facilitates industry** into that scheme. It does not print a second logo.

## What you still must not say

- That PHC itself is JAKIM.
- That a PHC–JGC MoU is an SPHM certificate.
- That a Sinotrans warehouse is “JAKIM certified” because PHC sat in a meeting.

Those three remain false. They are not a denial of Perak or of the Rulers.
