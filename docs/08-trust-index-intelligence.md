# Global Trust Index and Intelligence Framework

## Purpose

Provide evidence-based intelligence without turning regulatory certification into an arbitrary commercial score.

## Proposed dimensions

A trust/index model may separately measure:
- certification status;
- evidence completeness;
- provenance coverage;
- laboratory evidence quality;
- supply-chain continuity;
- recall/incident history;
- ESG evidence maturity;
- operational reliability;
- response performance.

The methodology must be transparent, versioned, auditable and appealable.

## Important separation

A commercial trust score must never be represented as equivalent to governmental certification. Certification status is an authoritative regulatory fact; analytics are an interpretation layer.

## Intelligence products

### Manufacturer
Demand signals, product performance, supply-chain exceptions and operational opportunities.

### Retailer
Stock, demand, expiry, recall and supplier intelligence.

### Regulator
Risk concentration, evidence exceptions, laboratory trends and compliance workflow intelligence.

### Consumer
Simple product trust/provenance information appropriate for public disclosure.

## Data governance

The system should use role-based access, aggregation, anonymization/pseudonymization where appropriate, purpose limitation and contractual controls. Confidential commercial data should not become a general-purpose data marketplace.

## Index governance

Create an independent methodology committee with representatives from relevant technical, regulatory, scientific, commercial and Shariah-governance perspectives. Publish methodology versions and change history.
