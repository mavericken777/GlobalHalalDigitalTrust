# ESG and Global Citizen Programme

## Purpose

Make consumers active participants in Tayyib-oriented responsibility, sustainability and circular-economy activity.

## Consumer journey

`purchase → scan → learn → use → recycle/return → verify action → receive recognition/reward`

Rewards should reinforce education and responsible action, not encourage manipulation or wasteful purchasing.

## Capabilities

- ESG product information;
- carbon/packaging awareness where data is verified;
- participating recycling-centre discovery;
- recycling/return verification;
- consumer education;
- gamified challenges;
- community-level impact reporting;
- transparent reward rules.

## Shariah/Tayyib framing

The programme can be designed around responsibility, stewardship, avoidance of harm and consideration for the wider community. Formal Shariah claims should be reviewed by qualified scholars or the relevant authority.

## Data ethics

Consumer participation data should be minimized, purpose-limited and protected. Public impact dashboards should prefer aggregated statistics. The platform should not create a hidden consumer-surveillance system.

## ESG evidence

Where sustainability claims are made, the platform should distinguish:
- verified evidence;
- supplier-declared information;
- calculated estimates;
- unverified claims.

This distinction is essential to prevent greenwashing.

## Global citizenship

The programme should teach that individual action can contribute to collective outcomes when participation scales globally. The measure of success is not points accumulated; it is verified awareness, participation and measurable environmental/social outcomes.
