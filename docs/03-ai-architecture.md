# AI Architecture and Four-Tier Onboarding

## Tier 1 — AI Knowledge Concierge

Purpose: remove friction before formal submission.

Capabilities:
- multilingual Q&A;
- Halal/Tayyib education;
- onboarding guidance;
- documentation checklists;
- short explanatory content and videos;
- explanation of roles, fees and next steps;
- escalation to human support.

The knowledge base must use approved terminology and distinguish authoritative policy from general guidance.

## Tier 2 — AI Process Advisor

Purpose: improve submission quality before regulatory review.

Capabilities:
- document extraction;
- missing-field detection;
- consistency checks;
- ingredient and supplier cross-reference;
- evidence readiness assessment;
- corrective-action guidance;
- workflow routing.

AI outputs should be explainable, logged and reviewable. It should not silently alter submitted evidence.

## Tier 3 — Human / Regulatory Verification

Purpose: preserve legitimate authority.

The system routes cases to authorized regulators, certification personnel, religious authorities or accredited laboratories as applicable. AI provides analysis and evidence context; authorized humans make decisions that require legal, religious or scientific authority.

## Tier 4 — AI Commercial and Quotation Engine

Purpose: convert readiness into commercial participation.

Capabilities:
- service-path recommendation;
- indicative pricing calculation;
- quotation preparation;
- expected timeline estimation;
- ROI/business-value explanation;
- onboarding conversion;
- partner/service matching.

Any pricing, regulatory or certification claim must be sourced from current approved rules and commercial schedules.

## Agentic AI controls

Agents should operate with:
- explicit scopes;
- least-privilege credentials;
- approval gates for consequential actions;
- immutable action logs;
- tool allowlists;
- human override;
- model/version traceability;
- prompt and policy governance;
- continuous evaluation.

## AI risk controls

AI must detect and flag hallucination risk, conflicting records, anomalous submissions, suspicious document changes, duplicate identities and unusual transaction patterns. High-impact decisions require human review.

## AI learning loop

`observe → classify → recommend → human/authorized action → outcome → evaluate → improve`

Training and evaluation datasets must respect confidentiality, jurisdictional controls, intellectual property and applicable privacy law.
