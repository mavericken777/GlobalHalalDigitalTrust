Working extract for trip use. **Not** a substitute for the signed scan. Do not paste this page to a counterparty as if it were the original.

## Parties

- Global Halal Supply Chain Limited, Hong Kong SAR (“GHSC HK”)
- Sinotrans Overseas Development Ltd., PRC (“Sinotrans Overseas”)

## Operative points (paraphrase)

1. Purpose: position Sinotrans Overseas as a Halal-compliant logistics/warehousing **partner-to-be**; GHSC supports audit, compliance structuring and MS 2400 (Parts 1–3) alignment; joint work on integrity/traceability China → GCC / global Halal markets.
2. Sinotrans identifies **selected** hubs/warehouses. GHSC supports readiness, training frameworks, certification **coordination**.
3. Data into AmanahGraph **subject to agreed technical protocols**.
4. GHSC may engage Halal authorities and customs; Green Lane is an objective, not a grant. Sinotrans “acknowledges” efficiency benefits.
5. Focal persons + optional joint steering.
6. **No** pricing, exclusivity, or revenue-sharing in this paper. Separate commercial documents required. Each side keeps commercial independence.
7. Confidentiality binds.
8. **Not** a partnership or JV except confidentiality.
9. Two years from signing unless ended by mutual written notice.
10. Governing law: Hong Kong SAR.
