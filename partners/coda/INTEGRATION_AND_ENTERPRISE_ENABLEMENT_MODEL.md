# CODA Integration & Enterprise Enablement Model

## Positioning

**Proposed role:** China Enterprise Mobilisation & Export Enablement Backbone for the Global Halal Digital Trust Ecosystem.

This is a strategic proposal for discussion. The exact legal mandate, institutional authority, programme mechanisms and eligibility criteria of CODA must be verified from documentary sources and/or directly with CODA before being represented externally as fact.

## What CODA should enable

CODA should be approached as the mechanism for finding, mobilising and organising qualified Chinese manufacturers and producers at scale, while GHSCL provides the Halal/Tayyib trust pathway and international transaction architecture.

`CODA enterprise network -> structured onboarding -> Halal readiness -> China laboratory evidence -> digital trust identity -> Platinum monitoring -> Halal logistics -> GCC market access -> B2B transaction`

## Division of responsibility

| Party | Primary responsibility | Key outputs |
|---|---|---|
| CODA | Enterprise mobilisation, institutional bridge, market development, introductions and support-programme exploration | Qualified enterprise pipeline, introductions, cohort mobilisation, support pathway |
| GHSCL | Digital Trust & Trade orchestration, onboarding, evidence architecture, ecosystem governance | Trust record, DPP, workflow, APIs, dashboards, transaction orchestration |
| JAKIM / competent authority interfaces | Halal certification/recognition decisions within lawful mandate | Certification/official determinations |
| Laboratory | Scientific testing and controlled reporting within its accreditation/authorization scope | Test reports, evidence references |
| Manufacturer/producer | Product/process data, corrective actions, compliance participation | SKU/batch evidence, manufacturing records |
| Sinotrans / logistics operator | Physical custody and logistics execution | Shipment events, custody records, transport/warehouse evidence |
| GCC importer/distributor | Destination-market requirements, receiving, customs and commercial execution | Import evidence, receiving confirmation, market activation |

## Manufacturer onboarding gates

### Gate 1 — Discovery

Collect legal entity, factory, product portfolio, target markets, certifications and export ambitions.

### Gate 2 — Qualification

Screen product category, raw-material risks, existing quality systems, traceability maturity, laboratory needs and GCC target-market suitability.

### Gate 3 — Evidence

Connect required laboratory tests and source evidence. Preserve the distinction between test evidence and Halal certification.

### Gate 4 — Halal/Tayyib readiness

Map the manufacturer's process to applicable Halal requirements and Tayyib/product-integrity controls. The platform can support evidence and workflow; it does not itself issue certification.

### Gate 5 — Digital onboarding

Assign manufacturer/site/product/SKU/batch identifiers, source references, evidence hashes, roles and permissions.

### Gate 6 — Platinum eligibility

Select the full-stack real-time monitoring profile appropriate to the product and logistics risk.

### Gate 7 — Market and logistics readiness

Match target GCC market, importer/distributor, route, warehouse, transport and receiving controls.

### Gate 8 — Pilot transaction

Execute a controlled transaction with complete evidence and exception handling.

### Gate 9 — Scale

Expand only after KPI, compliance, technical and commercial gates are passed.

## CODA support concept

The programme should investigate whether CODA or other applicable Chinese programmes can reduce manufacturer adoption costs. Until confirmed in writing, the following are **proposed support categories**, not promised subsidies:

- hardware and sensor deployment;
- gateway/connectivity/integration costs;
- digital onboarding and training;
- pilot transaction costs;
- enterprise process improvement;
- export-market enablement;
- eligible financing or support programmes.

The principle is simple: **do not make the manufacturer absorb the full cost of entering a new trust infrastructure before the commercial value is proven.**

## Platinum enablement concept

The preferred policy proposal is to treat the Platinum monitoring package as shared digital-export infrastructure where possible, rather than a discretionary add-on. Support could be structured around eligible implementation costs, subject to programme rules.

## Scale model

A standardised onboarding factory is required. The system must allow many manufacturers to enter the same core workflow without bespoke architecture each time.

Core reusable objects:
- enterprise;
- site/factory;
- product/SKU;
- batch/lot;
- ingredient/raw-material evidence;
- laboratory test;
- Halal certificate/reference;
- device;
- shipment;
- custody event;
- exception;
- corrective action;
- importer;
- transaction.

## Success KPIs

- qualified manufacturers screened;
- manufacturers reaching Gate 4;
- manufacturers with laboratory evidence connected;
- manufacturers with digital identities;
- Platinum devices deployed;
- manufacturers matched to GCC buyers;
- pilot shipments completed;
- first transactions completed;
- average onboarding cost and time;
- support funding or cost-sharing successfully activated where available;
- exception rate and resolution time;
- evidence completeness.
