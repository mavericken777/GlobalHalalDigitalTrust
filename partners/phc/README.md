# Perak Halal Corporation Sdn Bhd

**Status:** Perak **State Government GLC** for the Halal industry — local and global. Instrument on file: INST-002 (MoU with JGC HK, 27 Aug 2025), non-binding JV intent except confidentiality.

## Institutional position

PHC is not a private promoter. It is the State of Perak’s corporate vehicle to build the Halal industry at home and abroad, under a Federation where:

- Islam is the religion of the Federation;
- the Sultan of Perak is the Head of the religion of Islam in the State;
- MAIAMP aids and advises the Sultan;
- JAIPK administers State Halal files through the national MYeHALAL system;
- JAKIM is the federal backbone of the **one** nationwide Halal scheme (MPPHM, national logo, international recognition);
- MS standards are the single technical set.

Federal Islamic / Halal regulation and State Islamic / Shariah administration are one system, not two competing codes. Full map: `CHINA_TRIP_2026/MALAYSIA_HALAL_AUTHORITY_MAP.md`.

## What PHC does on this corridor

Industry development, investor and manufacturer onboarding in Perak, facilitation of compliance with the national scheme, trade and park work. JAKIM recorded a formal courtesy engagement with PHC at Majlis Halal Malaysia (8 May 2019) on Perak’s Halal development plan.

## What PHC does not become by signing INST-002

A second certifier. SPHM / MYeHALAL decisions stay inside the competent Halal authority scheme. The MoA/MoU with JGC is a commercial-intent paper, not a certificate.
