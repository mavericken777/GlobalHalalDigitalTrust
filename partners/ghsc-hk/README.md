# Global Halal Supply Chain Limited

Global Halal Supply Chain Limited is the international operating and digital-infrastructure vehicle for the Global Halal Digital Trust ecosystem.

Its target functions include China-to-GCC coordination, the 24/7 Command Center, partner-facing workflow orchestration, and operational monitoring. Live services and integrations are represented according to their actual implementation state.

Authority decisions, certification, sovereign customs release, and regulated finance decisions remain with the competent authorities and licensed counterparties.
