# Partner RACI & Integration Matrix — October 2026 Mission

## Operating rule

Every partner must have a defined job, input, output, system interface, KPI, decision boundary and escalation route. Partnership status is not inferred from a meeting or announcement.

| Partner / stakeholder | Job description | Inputs | Outputs | Digital interface | Decision boundary |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| GHSCL | Ecosystem orchestrator and Digital Trust & Trade layer | Partner data, evidence, requirements | Trusted records, workflows, APIs, dashboards, transaction orchestration | Trust platform/API/event layer | Does not issue government certification unless legally authorised |
| CODA | China enterprise mobilisation, institutional bridge, internationalisation/market-development support and programme exploration | Enterprise network, introductions, programme information | Qualified pipeline, cohort mobilisation, institutional pathway, support-programme options | Enterprise onboarding portal/API; pipeline records | Does not substitute for Halal certification authority or promise unverified subsidy |
| JAKIM / competent Halal authorities | Sovereign Halal certification/recognition decisions within mandate | Applications, audit evidence, legal documentation | Official decisions/certificates/recognition | Authorised regulatory exchange | Certification authority remains sovereign |

## Integration contract for every participant

Before a participant enters a production pilot, document:

1. legal entity and role;
2. authorised representative;
3. scope of work;
4. data contributed;
5. data ownership and retention;
6. evidence responsibility;
7. security requirements;
8. service levels/KPIs;
9. incident/escalation process;
10. commercial terms;
11. IP and confidentiality;
12. termination/transition provisions.

## Partner maturity stages

`Prospect -> Validated -> Pilot Participant -> Contracted -> Operational -> Scaled`

No external statement should skip these stages.
