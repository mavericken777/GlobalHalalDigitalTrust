# October 2026 Strategic Implementation Mission

## Mission classification

This is a six-day strategic implementation mission for the Global Halal Digital Trust Ecosystem. It is not a courtesy itinerary. The operating rule is:

> Every meeting must produce an executable outcome. Every executable outcome must feed the first controlled China-to-GCC Halal transaction.

## Five mission layers

1. **Institutional legitimacy:** Malaysia/JAKIM and relevant State religious authority interfaces with China and GCC counterparties through lawful, documented channels.
2. **Product pipeline:** Chinese manufacturers/producers enter a structured Halal/Tayyib readiness and export pathway.
3. **Halal logistics infrastructure:** Sinotrans is evaluated as a trusted logistics node and potential audited/certified Halal logistics operator where applicable.
4. **GCC market access:** qualified Chinese supply is linked to GCC importers/distributors and transaction pathways.
5. **Digital Trust & Trade:** product identity, certification references, laboratory evidence, custody events, logistics telemetry, transaction records and audit evidence are linked without centralising sovereign source data.

## Strategic target

The mission should establish the conditions for GHSCL's first fully controlled, traceable China-to-GCC Halal B2B transaction. The first transaction is the validation event for the operating model, not merely a sales transaction.

## Mission decision gates

### Gate A — Partner readiness

No partner is represented externally as endorsed, certified, contracted or formally participating until documentary evidence exists.

### Gate B — Product readiness

A SKU enters the controlled transaction pipeline only when identity, evidence, Halal status/reference, logistics requirements and target-market requirements are sufficiently documented.

### Gate C — Logistics readiness

The selected corridor must have defined custody controls, documentation, monitoring, exception handling and destination receiving controls.

### Gate D — Digital trust readiness

Critical events are identified, attributable, time-stamped, integrity-protected and linked to the appropriate sovereign source records.

### Gate E — First transaction

The first transaction proceeds only after legal/compliance, product evidence, logistics, commercial, data and destination requirements are approved by the designated owners.

## Operating principle

**Certificate != Trust.** A certificate is a compliance artefact. The trust layer must establish that the product/batch associated with the certificate is the product/batch that moved through the controlled supply chain and that the relevant conditions and handovers are evidenced.

For Halal/Tayyib specifically, certification remains a matter for the competent authority and applicable legal framework. The digital system provides supporting evidence, chain-of-custody, monitoring, exception management and auditability.
