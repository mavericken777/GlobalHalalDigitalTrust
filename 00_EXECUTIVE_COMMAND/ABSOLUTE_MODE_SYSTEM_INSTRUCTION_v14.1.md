# Current Platform Operating Model

Global Halal Supply Chain Limited develops and operates Amanah and the Global Halal Digital Trust digital infrastructure. AHTE connects the end-to-end assurance journey and provides continuous monitoring, evidence lineage, analytics and operational decision support.

## Current operating model

- Physical supply corridor: China → GCC direct.
- Authority integration: AHTE ⇄ Direct JAKIM API ⇄ JAKIM.
- PHC and JAKIM operate in parallel across Perak/state and federal Malaysian governance.
- JAKIM/JAIN/JAIM, muftis, scholars and authorised halal auditors decide certification award and revocation through their applicable processes.
- AI/ML assists evidence review, anomaly detection, predictive analytics and preemptive strategies; it does not make certification decisions.
- The platform monitors the complete certified-SKU and premises journey, including suppliers, laboratories, audits, production, warehouses, logistics, ports, GCC receiving, distribution and verification.

## Evidence and status

Evidence is attributable to an object, event, actor and timestamp. Corrections supersede earlier records while preserving history. Integrity proofs detect changes to recorded data; they do not establish the truth of an observation. Laboratory findings are interpreted with method, scope, sample identity and review context. `NOT_DETECTED ≠ HALAL`.

Authority certification status, platform assurance status, logistics custody, customs disposition and finance decisions are recorded as distinct source-owned states. The platform propagates verified source updates and does not fabricate external responses.

## Standards and updates

Use the current maintained register of applicable Malaysian/JAKIM instruments, MPPHM, MHMS, HAS, IHCS, protocols, circulars, laboratory methods and destination requirements. Keep source identity, edition and update date with each applicable record. Normative wording must match its authoritative source.

## Product domains

The platform covers onboarding; requirements and controls; product, SKU, premises, suppliers and materials; laboratory sampling and custody; smart audit; CAPA and re-verification; production and digital twins; Sinotrans warehouse/logistics custody and telemetry; ports/customs interfaces; GCC receiving through retail; Command Center monitoring; predictive and preemptive analytics; verification and recall support; Shariah finance and Takaful interfaces.

Connector interfaces remain complete across development, sandbox, authorization and production environments. Display a production state only when a real provider response has been verified.
