# AHTE / IQ300 operating doctrine

## Mission

AHTE connects and continuously monitors the complete halal assurance journey for certified premises and products/SKUs. The platform links source records, evidence, laboratories, audits, production, custody, logistics, destination operations, analytics and verification from China origin through GCC markets.

## Organisation and governance

Global Halal Supply Chain Limited operates the international digital-infrastructure layer. PHC and JAKIM work in parallel across Perak/state and federal Malaysian governance within the shared Islamic framework. JAKIM/JAIN/JAIM, muftis, scholars and authorised halal auditors make certification award and revocation decisions through their applicable processes. AHTE supplies monitoring, evidence and decision-support context and records verified outcomes.

## System topology

```text
AHTE ⇄ Direct JAKIM API ⇄ JAKIM
China → GCC direct
```

Malaysia is the governance, assurance and authority-connectivity plane. The physical corridor is China to GCC direct.

## End-to-end assurance journey

```text
Organisation and premises onboarding
→ product/SKU, formula, suppliers and materials
→ applicable Malaysian/JAKIM and destination requirements
→ premises controls, HCP/SCCP and evidence
→ laboratory sample, custody, method, QC, result and review
→ smart audit, findings, corrective action and re-verification
→ certification status from the responsible decision process
→ production, batch, warehouse and logistics custody
→ ports/customs and GCC receiving
→ distribution, retail, verification and recall support
→ real-time monitoring, analytics, predictions and preemptive strategies
```

## AI/ML role

AI/ML supports data processing, completeness review, anomaly detection, prediction, impact assessment and recommended preemptive action. Authorised people carry out assigned operational actions. Certification award and revocation remain with the named certification decision makers. Platform assessments and lab results are not certification decisions.

## Evidence and event model

Each evidence record links an object, event, actor, timestamp and integrity proof. Source provenance is preserved. Corrections supersede earlier records without erasing their history. Integrity proofs show whether recorded data changed; they do not establish whether the underlying observation is true. Laboratory results retain sample identity, method, scope and review. `NOT_DETECTED ≠ HALAL`.

Certification status, platform assurance, operational custody, customs disposition and finance decisions remain separate source-owned records. Verified changes flow to connected product, premises and supply-chain views with provenance.

## Standards applicability

Use the current maintained register for the complete applicable Malaysian/JAKIM framework, including MPPHM, MHMS, HAS, IHCS, protocols, circulars, Malaysian Standards, laboratory methods and destination requirements. Bind each applicable record to its source, edition and update metadata. Do not invent normative wording.

## Operational capabilities

The platform supports China producers and suppliers; facility, product and SKU onboarding; laboratories; auditors and certification records; production systems and IoT; Sinotrans warehouses and logistics; ports/customs; GCC importers, warehouses, distribution, retail and verification; Command Center monitoring; recall scope; Shariah finance and Takaful interfaces.

## Connector operation

Interfaces remain complete across development, sandbox, authorization and production environments. Display provider state only from a verified provider response. Keep the interface ready for connection while representing actual configuration accurately.
