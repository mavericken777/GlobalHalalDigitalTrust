# Second supplied PDF batch — source reconciliation (28 September 2026)

## Review method and publication boundary

Ten attached PDFs were opened, hashed, counted and cover/scope checked against their visible pages, with text-layer coverage measured throughout. The two fully scanned standards were OCR sampled on cover, contents and first normative pages; automatic text extraction yields zero characters in both. This is a document identity and scope review, **not** independent verification of every clause or the present legal status of any edition. [The inventory](source-holdings-2026-09-28.json) records the SHA-256, page count and extraction coverage. The supplied PDFs are not committed to this public repository: multiple covers carry single-user copying/networking restrictions and redistribution rights for the others have not been established. No verbatim standard content or illustrations are copied here.
