# 01 — DEPARTMENT-BY-DEPARTMENT RACI

## RACI key

**R** Responsible = performs the activity.  
**A** Accountable = owns the outcome/decision.  
**C** Consulted = provides required expertise.  
**I** Informed = receives controlled status information.

The RACI is organised by function rather than assuming a single organisation owns the complete corridor.

## G. RACI operating rules

1. Every high-impact event must have a named accountable actor and authenticated identity.
2. AHTE records responsibility and evidence but does not silently assume governmental authority.
3. Any activity with legal, customs, public-health, product-safety or certification consequences must route to the relevant competent authority gate.
4. A role may be Responsible in one jurisdiction and only Consulted in another.
5. RACI is versioned; changes create a governance event and re-evaluate affected workflows.
