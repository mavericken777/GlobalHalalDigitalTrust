# Certification review and decision catalogue

| Review / decision | Meaning | Typical trigger |
|---|---|---|
| Routine assurance review | Internal controls are monitored and evidence is connected to the relevant product, premises, batch or activity | routine operation |
| Evidence and audit review | Records, tests and audit findings are reviewed against applicable requirements | application, surveillance or material change |
| Specialist review | Sertu, specialised Shariah or technical matters are referred to qualified reviewers | applicable specialist issue |
| Certification decision | Authorised JAKIM/JAIN/JAIM decision makers, muftis, scholars and halal auditors perform their assigned decision roles | award, renewal, scope, suspension or revocation |
| Regulatory review | Separate legal or regulatory requirements are assessed by the responsible authority | statutory or destination-country requirement |

AI/ML, laboratory results and digital records support continuous monitoring, evidence review and recommendations. Certification decisions remain with authorised human decision makers.
