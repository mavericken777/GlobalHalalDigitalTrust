# IQ300 Source, Coverage and Gap Register

## Source-status principle

The repository distinguishes:

1. **Clause-level source-derived** - complete numbered clause structure available in the uploaded PDF.
2. **Clause-family source-derived** - compendium supplies structured summaries, but not the full licensed numbered subclause text.
3. **External verification required** - current authority status, scheme implementation, legal treatment, or laboratory method version must be checked against the authoritative source before freeze.
