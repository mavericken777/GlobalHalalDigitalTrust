# IQ300 Authority Gate Catalog

| Gate | Meaning | Typical trigger |
|---|---|---|
| AG-00 | No external authority decision implied | routine internal control |
| AG-10 | Certification evidence gate | application/audit evidence must be complete before certification workflow |
| AG-20 | Authority verification gate | sertu, specialised Shariah decision or formal verification |
| AG-30 | Certification decision gate | panel/competent authority determination |
| AG-40 | Surveillance / change gate | material change, surveillance, renewal, suspension or revocation |
| AG-50 | Regulatory/legal gate | another regulator, statutory requirement or scope issue |

AI, laboratory evidence and digital records may support a gate but do not replace the competent authority decision represented by the gate.
