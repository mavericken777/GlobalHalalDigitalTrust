# IQ300 Audit Test Library

- `DOC-01` Document review - policy, manual, procedure, specification or controlled record definition.
- `REC-01` Record sampling - select time-bounded records and verify completeness, legibility, identity and traceability.
- `SITE-01` On-site inspection - observe the actual state against the controlled requirement.
- `INT-01` Interview - confirm responsible personnel understand and perform the control.
- `TRACE-01` Forward/backward trace test - reconstruct a lot, asset or custody chain through records.
- `WIT-01` Witness test - observe an operational control or monitoring activity.
- `COMP-01` Competence verification - credentials, training, assessment and role authorization.
- `AUTH-01` Authority evidence check - confirm formal authority verification or decision where required.
- `LAB-01` Laboratory evidence check - method version, matrix applicability, controls, validity and report linkage; laboratory evidence does not itself establish Halal certification.
- `CHANGE-01` Change-control review - inspect change request, risk assessment, approval, implementation and re-verification.
- `RECALL-01` Withdrawal/recall simulation - demonstrate affected-lot identification, containment, notification and effectiveness verification.
