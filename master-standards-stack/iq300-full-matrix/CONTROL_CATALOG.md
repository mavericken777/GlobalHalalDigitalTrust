# IQ300 Control Catalog

| ID | Control family | Primary purpose |
|---|---|---|
| GOV-SYS | Governance and Halal Management System | policy, responsibility, committee, source control, management review |
| HCP-CONTAMINATION | Contamination / najs / sertu | prevent, contain and verify contamination events |
| HCP-CUSTODY | Custody and segregation | preserve halal identity through handling and transfer |
| HCP-TRACE | Traceability and records | reconstruct lots, events, evidence and custody relationships |
| HCP-PERSONNEL | Personnel competence and conduct | ensure competent, hygienic and controlled human interaction |
| HCP-FACILITY | Facility / equipment / sanitation | maintain physical environment and equipment integrity |
| HCP-SUPPLIER | Supplier / outsource control | assure external parties affecting halal integrity |
| HCP-ASSURANCE | Validation / verification / audit | test control effectiveness and evidence quality |
| HCP-OPERATIONS | Operational process control | control process-specific halal risks and interfaces |

Risk classes are configurable: Critical, High, Medium, Low. The final score must remain tied to the applicable standard, process and authority rules.
