# IQ300 Official Current Verification Addendum - 17 September 2026

## Purpose

This addendum freezes the externally verified public-source facts used by the IQ300 compliance and China -> GCC market-validation package. It is a verification layer, not a substitute for licensed normative standards, regulator instructions, certificates, contracts or transaction documents.

## 1. JAKIM / Malaysia

- JAKIM's Halal Malaysia portal currently lists MHMS 2020 and MPPHM 2020 as certification references and provides the MYeHALAL application route for domestic and international organisations.
- JAKIM's official 2020 annual report records the launch of MPPHM (Domestic) 2020 and MHMS 2020, links MPPHM 2020 to the Trade Descriptions Act 2011 framework, and distinguishes IHCS for micro/small industry from HAS for medium/large industry.
- JAKIM's current foreign-Halal-certification-body page states that recognised FHCBs are used to monitor/verify Halal status for relevant imported materials/products and that recognition is time-limited.
- JAKIM announced withdrawal of recognition of China Islamic Association as a recognised foreign Halal certification body effective 19 February 2025. Therefore the project must not treat a Chinese Halal certificate as JAKIM-recognised without checking the current recognised-body list and the exact issuer/product scope.

## 3. Saudi Arabia

- SFDA states that imported food must comply with applicable Saudi technical regulations and standards, and importers must have the necessary SFDA account/registration and food-item registration.
- SFDA's imported-food guidance identifies documentary, identity and physical examination at border inspection points.
- SFDA publishes halal certification guidance and states that halal slaughter certification is mandatory for shipments of meat, poultry and their products; other food categories can trigger halal-document requirements according to the applicable conditions, ingredients and claims.
- SFDA confirms that prepackaged food labelling and adjoining explanatory statements must be in Arabic; if another language is used, the information must match the Arabic text.
- SFDA also distinguishes a product Halal Certificate from a consignment certificate for Halal products.

## 4. United Arab Emirates

- UAE MoIAT states that the Halal National Mark is granted after conformity verification and applies to products, services or production systems within its scope.
- MoIAT identifies UAE.S 2055-1, UAE.S 2055-2, UAE.S 993, GSO 713, GSO 21, GSO 9 and related standards in its Halal programme.
- UAE government guidance states that imported and locally produced food must be registered in the national food accreditation/registration system before being handled in UAE markets, and food information must satisfy UAE requirements.

## 6. Non-negotiable evidence gates

A candidate is not approved for Shipment 001 until all applicable records exist:

`legal entity -> factory site -> SKU -> formula/ingredients -> Halal certificate + issuer + scope + validity -> destination label -> importer registration -> required SFDA/UAE/GCC regulatory evidence -> laboratory evidence where required -> logistics qualification -> PO -> batch identity -> container/seal -> custody events -> border documents -> destination release -> receiving -> post-arrival verification`.

## 7. Source rule

Public websites are discovery/primary-claim sources for manufacturer screening. Authority status, certification, destination approval and regulatory eligibility require the competent authority, issuer, importer or contractual evidence to be retained in the IQ300 Evidence Fabric.
