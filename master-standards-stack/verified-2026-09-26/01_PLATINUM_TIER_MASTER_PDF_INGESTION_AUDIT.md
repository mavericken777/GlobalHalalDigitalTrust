## 1. Executive adjudication

The PDF is a high-density compilation that usefully consolidates Malaysian halal standards, MPPHM/MHMS, protocol, fatwa, laboratory and audit material. It is **not safe to treat the whole document as authority-issued verbatim normative text** because its own source index and inline attribution mix:

- Department of Standards Malaysia / MySOL catalogue and previews;
- JAKIM portals/documents;
- licensed/public standards fragments;
- university-hosted copies/previews;
- Taqyid;
- al-Barakah;
- SmartHalal;
- academic papers;
- institutional repositories;
- theses;
- derived “critical-limit translations”; and
- author-generated universal audit criteria.

The correct treatment is therefore **claim-by-claim adjudication**, not wholesale promotion.
