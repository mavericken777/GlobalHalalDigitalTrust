# Supplied PDF review — 27 September 2026

## Method and boundary

Ten user supplied PDFs were opened, inventoried by SHA-256 and PDF page count, extracted for text and visually sampled. See [source holdings](source-holdings-2026-09-27.json). The three MS 2400 editions were indexed by numbered body headings after front matter, with 1-based PDF page locations. This inventory is a source navigation aid. It is not a legal determination, an official amendment check, a claim of certification, or a complete extraction of every obligation. The PDFs remain outside the public repository. The covers of the MS 2400 copies restrict copying and networking under named single user licences.

## Discrepancies and disposition

The old 187/201/225 control objects total 613 and omit five deeper numbered headings in **each** MS 2400 edition: `4.3.4.7.1.1`, `4.3.4.7.1.2`, `4.3.4.7.2.1`, `4.3.4.7.2.2`, `4.3.4.7.2.3`. The new 192/206/230 index totals 628 unique locators. A numbered heading count is not a count of individual duties. The old transport and retail gzip files fail integrity checks, and the old master tarball fails decompression. Their paths, hashes and errors remain in the [retirement record](../00_EXECUTIVE_COMMAND/artifact-quarantine-2026-09-26.json); the files have been removed from the current tree. The warehouse 201-object gzip decodes but remains a historical proposed control set, not a complete normative source or complete locator index.

The [replacement archive](iq300-full-matrix/source-index-2026-09-27/IQ300_MS2400_SOURCE_INDEX_2026-09-27.tar.gz) holds only the manifest and three JSON locator indexes. It does not recreate or claim identity with the damaged master package. Further production work needs authorized source access, clause-by-clause review of control intents, official procedure checks, signed evidence and the open travel/partner gates before release.
