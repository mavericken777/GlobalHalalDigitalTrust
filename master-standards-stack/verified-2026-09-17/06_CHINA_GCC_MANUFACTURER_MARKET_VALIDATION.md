# China -> GCC Manufacturer and Market-Validation Dossier

**Control date:** 17 September 2026  
**Corridor:** China -> GCC direct  
**Validation class:** public-market and manufacturer-primary-source validation completed; private documentary/certificate/importer/transaction gates remain external evidence gates.

## 1. Purpose and decision standard

This dossier converts the initial ten-product/ten-manufacturer discovery set into a controlled pre-qualification pipeline for Shipment 001.

A manufacturer is not `approved` because its website displays a halal logo, an ISO/BRC/HACCP mark or a Middle East export claim. Public information establishes discovery and commercial plausibility only. Shipment eligibility requires the exact legal entity, factory, SKU, formulation, certificate issuer/scope/validity, destination recognition, label, importer, commercial order and batch/shipment evidence.

## 2. GCC regulatory entry architecture

The GCC is not one importer approval. Each destination country retains its own food, halal, customs, labelling, product-registration and importer requirements. The project therefore uses a jurisdiction profile per destination.

### 2.1 Saudi Arabia

Current Saudi Food and Drug Authority controls require imported food to comply with applicable Saudi technical regulations/standards and to move through an eligible importer/account/product-registration framework. Border control can include documentary, identity and physical examination.

For meat, poultry and their products, SFDA currently states that a halal slaughter certificate is mandatory. Other categories remain subject to the product's ingredients, claims and applicable requirements. Prepacked-food information must comply with Saudi/GSO labelling requirements; Arabic information is mandatory under the applicable labelling framework.

**Saudi Shipment 001 gates:**

`Saudi importer legal eligibility -> SFDA account/importer profile -> exact food item registration where required -> HS/category confirmation -> label/Arabic compliance -> ingredient/additive conformity -> halal certificate/issuer/scope as applicable -> health/origin/lab documents as applicable -> customs/border documentation -> consignment/release`.

### 2.2 United Arab Emirates

UAE MoIAT administers the federal Halal Products Control framework and publishes the applicable halal standards family, including UAE.S 2055-1, UAE.S 2055-2, UAE.S 993 and related GSO standards. The Halal National Mark is a conformity mark within the MoIAT programme; it is not treated by this project as a blanket substitute for importer/product/category requirements.

MoIAT's live registered-Halal-certification-body list currently includes active China-based certification bodies. This is useful for sourcing, but the exact issuer, certificate field/scope, dates and product/factory coverage must be checked at qualification time.

**UAE Shipment 001 gates:**

`UAE importer -> food/product registration/municipality or competent food-authority requirement -> product/category conformity -> Arabic/approved label -> halal body recognition and exact certificate scope where applicable -> customs/import docs -> port/food-control release`.

### 2.3 Bahrain, Kuwait, Oman and Qatar

No Saudi or UAE approval is extrapolated to the remaining GCC states. For every target country the importer must supply a current destination checklist before Shipment 001 is committed:

`competent food authority -> importer licence -> product registration -> halal/issuer recognition -> label language/content -> additive/ingredient limits -> shelf-life rules -> health/origin certificates -> laboratory requirements -> border process -> customs/tariff/HS -> retail/distributor requirement`.

## 6. Manufacturer qualification dossier - mandatory request pack

Each candidate must provide a controlled data room containing:

### Legal and factory

- full Chinese legal entity name in Chinese/English;
- unified social credit/business licence information;
- registered and manufacturing addresses;
- factory ownership/relationship to exporter/trader;
- production licences and export eligibility as applicable;
- organisational chart and responsible commercial/QA/halal contacts.

### Product/SKU

- current product catalogue;
- proposed GCC SKUs and internal SKU codes;
- complete formula/BOM including processing aids, flavours, colours, carriers and compound ingredients;
- allergen statement;
- nutrition/specification;
- shelf life and storage;
- packaging sizes/materials;
- carton/pallet/container loading configuration;
- HS code proposed by supplier, subject to importer/customs verification.

### Halal

- signed current halal certificate;
- certificate issuer and accreditation/recognition evidence for target destination;
- exact certificate number, effective/expiry date;
- factory/site scope;
- exact product/SKU/category scope;
- high-risk ingredient certificates and supplier evidence;
- slaughter/animal-origin evidence where relevant;
- shared-line, cleaning and sertu controls where relevant.

### Quality/food safety

- current BRCGS/FSSC/ISO/HACCP certificates where claimed;
- audit scope/site/expiry;
- recent major findings/corrective-action summary where contractually available;
- COA/micro/chemical test profile;
- traceability and recall capability.

### GCC market-entry

- existing GCC/MENA importer/distributor references if the manufacturer elects to provide them;
- current approved Arabic/English artwork examples;
- Saudi/UAE product registrations previously held, if any;
- halal-certification-body acceptance evidence in destination jurisdiction;
- country-of-origin and manufacturing/expiry coding format;
- regulatory claims list.

### Commercial

- FOB/EXW/CIF quotations for target volume;
- MOQ by SKU/label format;
- sample cost;
- lead time;
- production capacity and peak-season constraint;
- payment terms;
- Incoterms;
- private-label/tooling/artwork charges;
- exclusivity/distribution conditions;
- product liability/recall process.

## 7. Evidence-status pipeline

`DISCOVERED -> PUBLIC-CLAIM-VERIFIED -> DATA-ROOM-RECEIVED -> LEGAL-ENTITY-VERIFIED -> FACTORY-VERIFIED -> HALAL-CERTIFICATE-VERIFIED -> MATERIAL/SKU-QUALIFIED -> DESTINATION-REGULATORY-QUALIFIED -> BUYER/IMPORTER-ACCEPTED -> COMMERCIAL-APPROVED -> PILOT-BATCH-APPROVED -> SHIPMENT-001-ELIGIBLE`.

No state is skipped.

## 8. Halal certificate validation workflow

For each certificate:

1. identify issuer exactly;
2. verify issuer against the target destination's current recognition/registration list where applicable;
3. verify issuer credential dates/status;
4. verify manufacturer legal entity and factory address;
5. verify exact product/category/SKU scope;
6. verify certificate number and validity;
7. validate whether high-risk ingredient/sub-supplier evidence is required;
8. obtain issuer confirmation or registry verification where feasible;
9. bind certificate to SKU/site in the Trust Graph;
10. create expiry/revocation watch.

A UAE-registered China-based HCB today does not prove that a candidate's existing certificate was issued by that body or covers the proposed SKU. The link must be evidenced.

## 9. Commercial market validation model

A SKU moves to buyer presentation only after the project can calculate:

`FOB/EXW + China inland + origin handling + freight + insurance + destination port/clearance + duty/tax + halal/regulatory/certification/document costs + importer/distributor margin + retailer/marketplace margin + promotion/returns + FX/finance cost = landed and channel economics`.

Commercial decisions must then incorporate:

- competing shelf price and pack size;
- distributor/retailer target margin;
- shelf life remaining at arrival;
- MOQ vs trial volume;
- container utilisation;
- repeat-order lead time;
- private-label vs manufacturer brand;
- promotional support;
- payment/credit terms;
- recall/liability allocation.

No manufacturer is labelled a `hotcake` based solely on category popularity. The only defensible Shipment 001 choice is the SKU that clears evidence, regulatory, buyer and economics gates simultaneously.

## 10. Shipment 001 recommended selection logic

The pilot should minimise unnecessary technical/regulatory complexity while still proving the AHTE trust thread. The decision engine should therefore compare eligible SKUs across:

`current valid halal evidence; low animal-derived/high-risk ingredient complexity; stable shelf life; ambient transport; complete manufacturer traceability; current GCC retail category existence; buyer/importer interest; manageable MOQ; acceptable landed margin; label readiness; destination registration lead time; container/pallet practicality`.

This is a criteria set, not an evaluative manufacturer ranking. Final SKU is selected by the project owners/importer/buyer after the documentary data room is complete.

## 12. Sinotrans pilot integration

Once a SKU becomes `SHIPMENT-001-ELIGIBLE`, the logistics qualification pack binds:

`manufacturer release -> loading location -> vehicle/container identity -> container cleanliness/history risk -> segregation -> seal -> export handoff -> port gate -> vessel/route -> telemetry where applicable -> destination port -> border inspection/hold/release -> receiving warehouse -> importer receipt`.

Applicable MS 2400 controls form the project assurance overlay. Destination law and actual carrier/warehouse certification status remain distinct evidence objects.
