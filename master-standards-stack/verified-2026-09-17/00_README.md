## Non-negotiable authority boundaries

- JAKIM/JAIN/MAIN certification decisions are not replaced by AHTE, AI, laboratory results, blockchain, QR/NFC/RFID, manufacturer declarations or audit-support tooling.
- Malaysian Standards are technical normative instruments. Certification operating requirements also depend on MPPHM, MHMS, protocols, circulars, fatwa and applicable law.
- MS 1900:2025 is an organisation-level Shariah-based QMS. It is not MHMS 2020 and does not itself create Malaysia Halal certification.
- Analytical standards such as MS 2627, MS 2627-2, MS 2809 and MS 2810 produce evidence; they do not independently establish halal status.
- Public manufacturer claims are screening evidence only. Shipment eligibility requires certificate/issuer/scope verification, destination compliance, importer approval and transaction evidence.
