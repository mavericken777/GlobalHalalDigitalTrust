# MS 2400-1/-2/-3 Clause-by-Clause Control, Evidence and Audit Manual

**Source basis:** supplied MS 2400-1:2019, MS 2400-2:2019 and MS 2400-3:2019 PDFs.  
**Control date:** 17 September 2026

## 0. Method

This document is a paraphrased clause-to-control mapping. It does not reproduce copyrighted normative text. Every requirement is converted into an operational pattern:

`Clause -> intent -> control -> HCP -> evidence -> audit test -> failure state -> corrective action -> re-verification -> trust-state effect`.

## 2. MS 2400-1:2019 - Transportation detailed map

### Clause 4

**4.1 Shariah:** maintain current rules for applicable transport operation.  
**4.2.1 Halal policy:** policy covers Halal integrity throughout the transport service.  
**4.2.2 Organization:** responsibility and authority are defined, including internal halal committee.  
**4.3.1:** controlled documentation and source identification.  
**4.3.2:** documented HMS procedures.  
**4.3.3:** validate HCPs.  
**4.3.4.1:** identify potential contaminants/precursors.  
**4.3.4.2:** define controls.  
**4.3.4.3:** determine HCPs.  
**4.3.4.4:** define monitoring.  
**4.3.4.5:** define corrective actions.  
**4.3.4.6:** define verification.  
**4.3.4.7:** document/retain records.  
**4.4:** maintain risk-plan summary.  
**4.5:** maintain information and communication controls.

### Clause 5

**5.1:** describe inbound/outbound transport services; keep current.  
**5.2:** complete and sufficiently detailed flow diagrams; verify on site.  
**5.3:** identify process sites, storage and personnel facilities.  
**5.4.1:** verify consignments received/transferred.  
**5.4.2:** validate next custodian's compliance.  
**5.4.3:** identify stakeholders/users.  
**5.4.3.1-.3:** identify delivery actors, inform outsourced parties of Halal requirements and control handover activities.  
**5.4.4:** protect goods in transit, including agreed environmental conditions.  
**5.4.5:** control preparation/dispatch and agreements with next custodian.  
**5.4.6:** control loss/damage.  
**5.4.7:** maintain documentation/evidence.  
**5.4.8:** control agents/outsourcing parties.

### Clause 6

**6.1:** operate the approved transport chain service.  
**6.2:** keep operational records.  
**6.3.1:** contain and disposition contaminated/affected goods.  
**6.3.2:** identify, isolate and evaluate doubtful goods; release only through controlled evaluation.  
**6.3.3:** execute and record corrective action.  
**6.4.1-.6:** isolate/notify promptly, secure affected goods, document cause/extent/result and verify effectiveness.  
**6.5:** communication and escalation.  
**6.6.1-.3:** maintain inbound/outbound traceability and define retention.  
**6.7.1-.4:** control monitoring/measuring equipment; calibration/verification basis and records.  
**6.8:** emergency preparedness.  
**6.9.1-.3:** control outsourced processes, evaluate/select and re-evaluate providers.

### Clause 7

**7.1:** transport location.  
**7.2:** premises design/layout.  
**7.3:** facilities/equipment/materials; identifiable containers; hygiene facilities; suitability of equipment/materials.  
**7.4:** personnel hygiene, health, cleanliness, wounds, handwashing, contact with non-halal goods, conduct and visitor controls.  
**7.5:** environment/perimeter/grounds.  
**7.6:** equipment maintenance.  
**7.7:** cleaning/sanitation; vehicle cleaning after delivery.  
**7.8:** sertu when applicable, supervised/verified by competent authority as required, recorded; use current prescribed method.  
**7.9:** drainage/waste.  
**7.10:** training.  
**7.11:** physical/chemical/biological contamination and pest control.

### Clause 8

**8.1:** internal halal audit.  
**8.2:** management review.  
**8.3:** complaints and feedback.  
**8.4:** responsiveness to change and controlled updates.

## 6. Audit evidence minimums

Each HCP record must capture `HCP_ID, rule_version, object_id, lot/batch, location, actor, time, control result, evidence references, exception flag, verifier`.

## 7. Failure-state model

`PENDING -> CONTROLLED -> VERIFIED`  
Exceptions: `HOLD / QUARANTINED / NCR / CAR / RE-VERIFICATION / DISPUTED / EXPIRED / REVOKED / RECALLED`.

A platform state must never be described as an official halal certification credential unless linked to an authority-issued credential.

## 8. Traceability test

A valid trace test must prove both directions:

`input -> process -> lot -> pallet -> container -> custody -> warehouse -> retailer`  
and  
`consumer/retail lot -> pallet/container -> custody -> warehouse -> manufacturer inputs`.

## 9. Audit checklist closure rule

A clause is **Closed** only where evidence is linked, current, attributable and testable. A clause is **Open** if it is merely documented without operational proof. A clause is **Blocked** when its governing authority decision or external evidence is absent.
