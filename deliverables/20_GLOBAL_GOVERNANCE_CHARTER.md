# Global Halal Digital Trust Governance Charter

## Purpose

Create a governance framework that permits global interoperability without centralizing sovereign authority.

## Governing layers

### Global Trust Council
Sets common technical trust, interoperability, evidence and security policies.

### National Authority Council
Represents participating jurisdictions and protects national regulatory/data sovereignty.

### Scientific & Laboratory Council
Coordinates evidence, laboratory interoperability and technical testing governance without overriding national accreditation.

### Shariah Advisory Council
Provides Shariah/Tayyib guidance and interpretation within its mandate. AI remains advisory.

### Commercial Advisory Council
Represents manufacturers, logistics providers, retailers, financiers and other ecosystem users.

### Consumer & ESG Council
Represents user trust, transparency, accessibility and sustainability participation.

## Governance rules

- no jurisdiction loses legal authority by joining;
- no participant can unilaterally control the global trust layer;
- conflicts of interest must be disclosed;
- certification decisions remain with authorized bodies;
- evidence and scoring methodologies are versioned;
- disputes have an appeals path;
- material policy changes require documented governance approval.

## Transparency

Publish:
- policy versions;
- trust framework revisions;
- scoring methodology;
- governance decisions where legally permissible;
- material incidents after appropriate security/legal review;
- independent assurance reports.

## Accountability

Every material action has an accountable organisation/system identity and evidence record.
