# Global Halal Trust Index & Intelligence Exchange

## Purpose

Create a transparent, evidence-based intelligence layer for manufacturers, retailers, regulators and consumers without turning the platform into an opaque ranking system.

## Product identity

Every product can have a persistent digital identity linked to approved provenance and evidence events.

## Trust index dimensions

Possible dimensions, subject to governance approval:
- regulatory status;
- evidence completeness;
- laboratory evidence status;
- supply-chain traceability;
- audit/corrective-action history;
- logistics integrity;
- retailer receiving integrity;
- ESG evidence;
- complaint/issue resolution;
- evidence freshness.

Weights must be published, versioned and auditable.

## Score governance

The score must:
- distinguish regulatory certification from commercial analytics;
- show evidence freshness;
- allow correction/appeal;
- avoid discriminatory or anti-competitive effects;
- protect confidential information;
- preserve methodological transparency.

## Intelligence exchange

Authorized participants receive intelligence appropriate to their role:

**Manufacturers:** demand, supplier and operational intelligence.

**Retailers:** stock, demand, product and supplier intelligence.

**Regulators:** compliance, evidence and risk intelligence.

**Consumers:** verified product/provenance information.

**Financiers:** authorized evidence/risk data for financing decisions.

## Data principle

The platform monetizes trusted intelligence and services where lawful, but must not sell confidential personal or commercially sensitive records contrary to governance obligations.

## Analytic products

- Market Demand Pulse
- Halal Supply Risk Monitor
- Supplier Performance Monitor
- Retail Availability Monitor
- ESG Participation Index
- Traceability Coverage Index
- Evidence Readiness Score
- Corridor Risk Dashboard
