# Source Register, Claims & Verification Rules

## Purpose

This register separates source-derived requirements from strategic proposals so that the programme can be presented credibly to governments, investors, regulators and commercial partners.

## Uploaded project sources

1. `MS 2400-1:2019` — Malaysian Standard: Halal supply chain management system, Part 1: Transportation — General requirements.
2. `MS 2400-2:2019` — Malaysian Standard: Halal supply chain management system, Part 2: Warehousing — General requirements.
3. `MS 2400-3:2019` — Malaysian Standard: Halal supply chain management system, Part 3: Retailing — General requirements.
4. `halal audit.pdf` — supplied Halal audit training material.
5. `halal.pdf` — supplied Halal awareness training material.

## Source-derived themes

### MS 2400-1
The transportation standard provides requirements around Shariah-related conformity, documented Halal policy, internal Halal committee responsibilities, traceable records, source identification, Halal Risk Management, contamination/precursor identification, control measures, monitoring, verification, corrective action, recalls/withdrawals and document/record control.

### MS 2400-2
The warehousing standard is the source basis for warehouse integrity, storage/handling controls, segregation/separation, condition monitoring, documentation and outsourced-party controls.

### MS 2400-3
The retail standard is the source basis for supplier monitoring, receiving controls, storage conditions, separation/identification, preparation/dispatch, traceability-related documentation, proof of delivery and retail record controls.

### Halal audit training material
The supplied audit training describes document review, on-site inspection, confidentiality, evidence gathering, non-conformance reporting and corrective-action follow-up. It references company profile, raw ingredients, employees, premise cleanliness, processing, equipment/utensils, packaging/labelling, storage/handling, transportation/distribution and waste management.

## Current external sources used for positioning

### Sinotrans official
Sinotrans publicly describes integrated logistics services, digital logistics information systems, EDI, IoT, Big Data, Cloud Computing, AI and Blockchain applications, as well as its Y2T platform and global logistics services. Official sources: https://www.sinotrans.com/ and https://online.sinotrans.com/ .

### JAKIM official
JAKIM's public Halal Status Check describes the Malaysian Halal Directory as a source for domestic and foreign Halal certification information. https://www.islam.gov.my/en/law-legal/halal-status-check .

JAKIM also publishes announcements concerning recognition of foreign Halal certification bodies. https://www.halal.gov.my/ .

## Claims requiring formal verification before external publication

- Any specific government laboratory authorization status.
- Any specific JAKIM endorsement of a named Chinese laboratory.
- Any product-assurance compensation/insurance commitment.
- Any use of JAKIM or other governmental certification marks.
- Any proposed royalty, licensing, participation fee or revenue-sharing arrangement with a regulator/government.
- Any ownership/shareholding arrangement not evidenced by signed corporate documents.
- Any statement that a specific government department has formally agreed to the programme.
- Any statement that a specific partner has signed a commercial agreement.

## Presentation rule

Never convert a project discussion, proposal, target or verbal understanding into a factual public claim without an authoritative source or signed document.
