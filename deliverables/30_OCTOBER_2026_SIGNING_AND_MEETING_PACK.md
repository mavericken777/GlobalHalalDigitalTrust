## Academy and product claims

“Using JAKIM certification to improve international food safety testing” should be clarified as **cooperation on analytical evidence supporting applicable halal assurance and food safety workflows, subject to competent-authority requirements**. No meeting participant can confer JAKIM authority by agreement alone.

For Blue Diamond / Bluamec / Deposon / Ka Shui, obtain:

1. Registered legal names, factory/site addresses, trading/manufacturing relationships and product ownership/licensing.
2. Exact SKU, formulation disclosure through a controlled channel, SDS, batch COA, stability and traceability records.
3. Accredited test method and scope, organism/contact-time/concentration/surface conditions, independent efficacy reports and residue/material compatibility.
4. Evidence behind “alcohol-free” and any certification claim, including issuer, scope, validity and product match.
5. Intended-use and market-specific acceptance pathway; a disinfectant is not automatically food-contact approved, halal-certified or a replacement for sertu.
6. Factory QA records, production capacity evidence, change control, complaint/recall process and proposed sampling trial.

Keep laboratory results separate from halal decisions: **NOT DETECTED != HALAL**. Keep ordinary cleaning, disinfection and authority-governed sertu distinct. No product endorsement is created by the demonstration.
