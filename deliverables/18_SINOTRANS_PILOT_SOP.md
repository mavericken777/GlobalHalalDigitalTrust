# Sinotrans Pilot SOP

## Pilot objective

Demonstrate that a China-to-Malaysia Halal logistics corridor can exchange trusted logistics evidence without replacing existing operational systems or centralizing sensitive national data.

## Roles

- Manufacturer: source product and batch evidence.
- Laboratory: authorized scientific evidence where in scope.
- Sinotrans: logistics and custody events.
- Warehouse: receiving/storage/dispatch evidence.
- Retailer/importer: destination integrity and receiving events.
- Platform operator: trust orchestration, evidence hashing and dashboards.
- Competent authority: regulatory decision-making where formally participating.

## Pre-flight checklist

- contractual scope approved;
- data-sharing policy approved;
- security architecture reviewed;
- participant identities established;
- test product/batch defined;
- API endpoints tested;
- evidence retention class defined;
- incident contacts confirmed.

## Live shipment procedure

1. Register product and batch.
2. Attach authorized origin/laboratory evidence references.
3. Create shipment and container identities.
4. Record pickup and loading.
5. Record handovers and transport milestones.
6. Record relevant environmental telemetry.
7. Record port/customs documentation references.
8. Record warehouse receipt and conditions.
9. Record dispatch and delivery.
10. Generate final evidence manifest.
11. Validate hash chain.
12. Produce regulator/enterprise evidence pack.

## Exception procedure

For any exception:
1. freeze/flag the affected event or shipment as policy requires;
2. preserve evidence;
3. record the exception;
4. notify authorized parties;
5. perform risk assessment;
6. implement corrective action;
7. record verification;
8. release/close status only through authorized workflow.

## Acceptance criteria

A pilot is successful if:
- at least 95% of in-scope critical events are digitally evidenced;
- no unauthorized cross-border data is transferred;
- evidence hashes validate after synchronization;
- authorized users can reconstruct the shipment chain;
- exceptions are recorded without destructive overwrite;
- audit preparation time is materially reduced;
- existing Sinotrans operating systems continue to function normally.

Targets are proposed pilot KPIs, not established contractual commitments.
