# Global Halal ESG & Global Citizen Framework

## Purpose

Connect Halal Tayyib values with practical environmental, social and governance participation across the product lifecycle.

## Consumer journey

`Purchase -> Scan -> Learn -> Act -> Verify -> Reward -> Understand collective impact`

## Educational focus

The platform should explain why responsible behaviour matters. Rewards are an engagement mechanism, not the objective. The intended outcome is awareness, conviction and constructive participation.

## Circular economy module

Participating consumers can:
- identify recyclable packaging;
- locate participating recycling centres;
- receive verified drop-off instructions;
- record voluntary recycling actions;
- view aggregate impact.

## Points model

Points should reward verified actions, not merely purchases. Anti-fraud measures are required. Points must not be presented as a religious judgement or a substitute for faith-based practice.

## ESG data

Potential indicators:
- packaging material and recyclability;
- transport mode and relevant emissions factors;
- energy/water data where available;
- waste/recovery;
- supplier social indicators;
- governance/control indicators;
- community participation.

Claims should be evidence-based and clearly label estimates.

## Global citizen dashboard

Users can view:
- personal participation;
- community participation;
- aggregated recycling outcomes;
- educational progress;
- participating locations.

Do not expose personally identifying information without lawful purpose and consent/other legal basis.

## Shariah/Tayyib framing

The ecosystem can teach that responsible stewardship, cleanliness, avoidance of harm and consideration for others are compatible with Halal Tayyib objectives. Religious interpretations and formal rulings must come from competent authorities rather than from an AI scoring model.
