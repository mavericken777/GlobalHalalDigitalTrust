# Retailer & Importer Playbook

## Objective

Make the retailer/importer a trusted verification and intelligence node rather than the last passive step in the chain.

## Core workflows

### Supplier onboarding
- verify supplier identity;
- validate product and Halal evidence references;
- record approved supplier status;
- establish service-level and evidence expectations.

### Receiving

Record:
- shipment/product identity;
- batch/lot;
- quantities;
- receiving time;
- condition;
- documentation references;
- exception/shortage/damage.

### Storage

Support the supplied MS 2400-3 retail controls for storage conditions, handling, segregation/separation, identification, documentation and proof of delivery/transfer.

### Retail intelligence

AI services:
- demand forecasting;
- low-stock detection;
- expiry-risk alerts;
- recall alerts;
- supplier performance;
- Halal evidence completeness;
- product trust index;
- ESG signals.

### Consumer scan

Consumer view should show:
- product identity;
- verified status;
- provenance milestones;
- relevant Halal evidence status;
- ESG information;
- recycling options;
- issue/feedback pathway.

## Multi-tenant data governance

A retailer must not automatically see another retailer's confidential sales data. Aggregated intelligence must use explicit governance and privacy controls.

## Recall

When a regulatory/quality event occurs:
1. identify affected batches;
2. identify locations and shipments;
3. notify authorized stakeholders;
4. preserve evidence;
5. record action and disposition;
6. update the public/retailer status as authorized.

## KPI dashboard

- evidence completeness;
- receiving discrepancies;
- recall trace time;
- stockout rate;
- expiry loss;
- supplier exception rate;
- consumer scan volume;
- ESG participation;
- corrective-action closure.
