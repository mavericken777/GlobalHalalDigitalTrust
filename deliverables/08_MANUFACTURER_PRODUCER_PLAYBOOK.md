# Manufacturer & Producer Playbook

## Proposition

The platform should give manufacturers measurable operational value before asking them to adopt additional compliance workflows.

## Journey

`Discover -> Learn -> Readiness -> Quote -> Submit -> Correct -> Verify -> Produce -> Ship -> Monitor -> Improve`

## Onboarding pack

The AI asks for and maps:
- company profile;
- facilities;
- products;
- ingredients/raw materials;
- supplier/manufacturer identities;
- product specifications;
- Halal certificates where applicable;
- laboratory reports where applicable;
- packaging information;
- process flow;
- storage/transport arrangements;
- traceability capability;
- internal Halal assurance controls.

These categories reflect the supplied Halal audit and awareness training material and should be mapped to the authoritative current procedure used for the specific application.

## Readiness score

Score dimensions can include:
- identity completeness;
- ingredient evidence completeness;
- supplier evidence;
- process controls;
- traceability;
- laboratory evidence;
- logistics readiness;
- documentation control;
- corrective action status.

A score is an internal readiness aid, not a certification decision.

## Operational intelligence

The platform can provide authorized analytics for:
- demand;
- inventory;
- waste;
- supplier performance;
- cycle time;
- exception frequency;
- logistics cost;
- quality signals;
- ESG indicators.

Competitor-confidential information must never be disclosed merely because the platform has aggregated data.

## Commercial onboarding

The quotation engine should show:
- selected services;
- assumptions;
- one-time implementation fees;
- recurring services;
- optional laboratory/logistics/ESG modules;
- expected milestones;
- governance and support model.

## Continuous improvement

Use the evidence graph and operational analytics to identify recurring issues, then create corrective/preventive action plans. The platform should aim to help the manufacturer stay ready, rather than preparing only when an audit is imminent.
