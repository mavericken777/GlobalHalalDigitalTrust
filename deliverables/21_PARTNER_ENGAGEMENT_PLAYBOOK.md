# Partner Engagement Playbook

## Objective

Create a repeatable path for government, laboratory, logistics, manufacturer, retailer, financial and technology participants.

## Government

Lead with sovereignty, evidence, regulatory interoperability, security and reduced administrative burden. Do not request transfer of regulatory authority.

## Laboratory

Lead with scientific evidence integrity, chain of custody, secure digital reporting, international visibility and commercial ecosystem reach. Keep laboratory accreditation/authorization legally separate from platform participation.

## Logistics provider / Sinotrans

Lead with differentiated trusted logistics, evidence automation, reduced audit preparation, new Halal trade corridors and value-added digital services.

## Manufacturer

Lead with faster onboarding, multilingual knowledge, evidence reuse, operational intelligence, cost visibility and market access.

## Retailer

Lead with inventory intelligence, recall traceability, product trust, supplier visibility and consumer confidence.

## Financier

Lead with high-integrity evidence, supply-chain visibility, risk indicators and ESG information. Any financing product must be legally reviewed and Shariah-reviewed where claimed as Shariah-compliant.

## Consumer

Lead with simple scanning, transparent provenance, education and meaningful participation.

## Partnership stages

`Discovery -> NDA/confidentiality where appropriate -> process mapping -> governance review -> technical validation -> pilot -> assurance -> commercial agreement -> scale`

## Non-negotiable

No marketing document should describe a prospective partner as already endorsed, certified, contracted or formally participating until documentary evidence exists.
